# LXGW WenKai Screen

`LXGWWenKaiScreen.ttf` is the unmodified regular screen-reading font from
[LXGW WenKai Screen v1.522](https://github.com/lxgw/LxgwWenKai-Screen/releases/tag/v1.522).
Its SHA-256 is
`cd1a6fa39c4ea42fd8f4e289945789b0e510cf7016435640f8893cdad9b220f3`.

under SIL Open Font License 1.1. It is bundled without modification so the
reader's 楷 typeface has the same Chinese glyphs on every supported platform.
